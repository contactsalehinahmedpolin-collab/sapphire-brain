# Sapphire Lead: Positioning (single source of truth; every agent reads this first)
Updated 10 Nov 2026 · Owner: Polin · Maintained by: Claude

## One line
We help founder-run health and wellness brands whose marketing got stuck in the past turn the demand they already have into more orders and more margin: a modern funnel and ads on the front end, factory-direct sourcing from Guangdong on the back end.

## Who we serve (ICP, modelled on Nature Cast)
- Founder or president-run US brands, roughly $300K–$3M, 1–15 staff. The founder reads their own email.
- Health, supplements, pain and joint relief, men's health, anti-aging, natural remedies, pet health, senior health, fitness programs.
- Demand exists (email list, catalog, radio/TV/print, repeat buyers) but the funnel is dated: old site or copyright, fax or phone orders, no reviews, no subscription, few or old Meta ads, catalog split across domains.
- Physical products that can be made or packed in Asia (capsules, bottles, pouches, devices, DVDs/kits).
- Not for: big brands with in-house teams or agencies, funding over $10M, 20+ fresh ads.

## The problem we name
"You built real demand the hard way, but the store, the ads and the follow-up are from 2015. Every month that costs you orders you've already paid for, and margin you're giving to your supplier."

## The offer: Profit Sprint (no-brainer)
- Outcome: more orders and more margin per order in 30 days.
- Proof before payment: a free 3-fix audit (one order fix, one margin fix from our Guangdong factories, one repeat-order fix) plus a 3-minute Loom.
- Speed: the new page, flows and first ads go live in 14 days.
- Effort: done for you; the founder only approves.
- Risk reversal: if the Sprint doesn't find value worth more than the first month's fee, they don't pay for month 2.
- Price (Polin to confirm): founding clients $1,500 for the first month (first 3 clients), then $3,500 a month.

## Proof we may state (nothing else)
- VitalMax / NeuroFlex topical pain spray: full DTC marketing infrastructure (ad scripts, UGC system, landing pages, Meta roadmap, advertorial bridge pages).
- Dr. Roni DeLuz: practitioner websites. MedRevIQ: website rebuild.
- Guangdong-registered company with direct factory access.
No invented numbers or clients, ever.

## Voice
One founder writing to another. Plain, warm, specific, under 80 words. Respect older operators: their old site is an opportunity, never a joke. No links in cold emails. Ask for a one-word "yes".

## Daily operating numbers
- 40 first emails a day from the warmed inboxes (from 16 Nov), 9–11am in the recipient's time zone, Monday to Friday.
- Pipeline floor: at least 120 leads in review or ready (3 days of sending). Target 150.
- Every lead must be researched (research/<lead_id>.md) before it can be sent.
