# Brolly Sheets — Diane Hurford (niche 8, NZ/AU)
**Snapshot:** Waterproof bed protection brand founded about 20 years ago in Diane Hurford's garage. She is still founder and CEO. Products are designed in NZ and made at a small rural factory in China. The adult/senior line Independently You launched in late 2019 (bed sheets, chair and car-seat protectors, adult bibs, washable adult briefs). Stocked in NZ, AU and the UK (Fledglings).
