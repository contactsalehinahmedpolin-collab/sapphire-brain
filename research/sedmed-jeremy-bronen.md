# SedMed / Jeremy Bronen (niche 8)

**Snapshot**
- Woodbridge, Connecticut maker of the SedMed Toilet Lift Assist: a non-electric, gas-spring toilet lift that helps people sit and stand. No battery or motor. ([UConn](https://today.uconn.edu/2023/05/alumnus-startup-helps-prevent-falls-associated-with-toileting), [PRWeb](https://www.prweb.com/releases/sedmed-and-montefiore-einstein-wakefield-campus-launch-qi-study-to-measure-impact-of-toilet-lift-assist-in-preventing-falls-staff-injuries-during-toilet-transfers-302351502.html))
- Jeremy Bronen started it as a UConn senior design project in 2020. He has been sole founder since his co-founder left in January 2023, and he is CEO. ([UConn](https://today.uconn.edu/2023/05/alumnus-startup-helps-prevent-falls-associated-with-toileting), [Fox61](https://www.fox61.com/article/news/local/new-haven-county/connecticut-company-toilet-lift-assist-prevent-bathroom-falls-sed-med/520-2a4f57d9-6fda-4ca4-ac7f-8a115269b8af))
- First sale was May 2023. Retail price is about $1,199. Before this round it had raised over $1M, including a $931K seed led by Connecticut Innovations. ([UConn](https://today.uconn.edu/2023/05/alumnus-startup-helps-prevent-falls-associated-with-toileting))
- Used in more than 40 hospital systems. Positioned for private homes, senior living and healthcare. ([StartupResearcher](https://www.startupresearcher.com/news/108-biocapital-leads-usd4-million-investment-in-sedmed))

**Moment of need**
- October 1, 2026: a $4M round led by 108 BioCapital to speed up commercial expansion and grow distribution and channel partnerships. ([StartupResearcher](https://www.startupresearcher.com/news/108-biocapital-leads-usd4-million-investment-in-sedmed), [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/158403-sedmed-raises-4m-to-scale-non-electric-toilet-lift-for-mobility-limited/))
- The sources describe hospital and partner channels, but no direct-to-consumer channel. The adult child buying for a parent at home is not being marketed to.
- Scaling from batches of about 250 units to wider distribution makes per-unit component and carton costs matter. ([UConn](https://today.uconn.edu/2023/05/alumnus-startup-helps-prevent-falls-associated-with-toileting))

**3 fixes**
1. Written factory quote on the gas springs, hardware kit and shipping carton/inserts, next to current cost.
2. A home-buyer page aimed at the adult child: a fit checker (toilet type, 70-275 lb user range), an install video and a "buy for Mom/Dad" flow.
3. Post-purchase email: install check-in, a 30-day review ask and referrals to senior living communities.

**Watch-outs**
- Mostly B2B/hospital today, so check whether home DTC is a real priority after the round.
- US brand. The hardware sourcing pitch has tariff exposure, so lead with packaging and DTC.
- No health or fall-reduction claims in copy.
