# Doctor Rogers Skin Care / Dr. Heather D. Rogers (niche 2)

**Snapshot**
- Founder: Dr. Heather D. Rogers, MD, double board-certified dermatologist; co-founder of Modern Dermatology in Seattle, where she sees patients full-time (https://www.doctorrogers.com/pages/founder).
- Started with Restore Healing Balm after patients reacted to common ointments; grew into Doctor Rogers Skin Care (founder page).
- Beauty Independent (undated rebrand piece): $1.5M annual sales, self-funded, channel split Amazon 40% / site 40% / retail 20%; retail at Cos Bar, Dermstore, Violet Grey, Credo; ~1.5M Healing Balm units sold; site customer return rate above 50% (https://www.beautyindependent.com/dermatologist-founded-brand-doctor-rogers-skin-care-unveils-elevated-redesign/).
- Dermstore lists balm 0.5 oz and 4 oz, lip balm, face cream and treatments, with refill versions of the face cream and Day/Night treatments (https://www.dermstore.com/doctor-rogers-restore-restore-healing-balm-4-oz./12901098.html).

**Moment of need**
- Refill SKUs (face cream, Day Preventive, Night Repair) only pay off if the refill pack is much cheaper than the full container (Dermstore listing above).
- Heavy Amazon share means fee drag on every unit; lowering packaging COGS protects margin (Beauty Independent above).
- No dated trigger in the last 90 days was found.

**3 fixes**
1. Written factory quote on balm containers, lip tubes, 30 ml treatment bottles and refill pods next to current cost.
2. Refill-reminder email/SMS flow at day 45-60 for treatment buyers, pointing to the site refill, not Amazon.
3. Practice-to-web bridge: a card in the Modern Dermatology checkout bag with a refill code so patients reorder direct.

**Watch-outs**
- Beauty Independent article is undated; the $1.5M figure may be several years old.
- Dermatologist brand: keep copy free of any skin-condition claims.
