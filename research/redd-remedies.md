# Redd Remedies: research
researched: 2026-11-10 by Claude · depth: search snippets only (site fetch blocked) · person confirmed: yes

## Snapshot
- Herbal supplement brand, Bradley, IL. Founder Dan Chapman (confirmed: podcast "Dan Chapman, Founder of Redd Remedies", whole Earth/Veganish and others).
- Sells mainly through health food stores and resellers (Vitacost, Fruitful Yield), plus its own Shopify site with an affiliate and ambassador program.
- Sources: https://vitaminretailer.com/2015-product-sales-training-manual/2015-product-sales-training-manual-redd-remedies/ · https://www.vitacost.com/collections/redd-remedies · https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0278/5781/3642/files/Redd_Remedies_Affiliate_and_Ambassador_Guide.pdf

## What's broken right now
- Demand is created by Dan's podcast tour (10+ shows), but listeners are sent to stores and resellers, so Redd doesn't capture the buyer. (podcast results above; Vitacost listing)
- Retail-first brand: the reseller sells the second bottle, not Redd. No evidence of a reorder or subscribe offer in search results. HYPOTHESIS until the site is checked.

## What it costs them
- Every podcast listener who buys at Vitacost is a customer Redd never emails again, at reseller margin.

## Our 3 fixes
1. Order: a podcast landing page ("Listened to Dan? Start here") with a stress-type quiz and a first-order bundle.
2. Margin: a factory-direct quote on capsules and amber bottles from Guangdong.
3. Repeat: a 30/60-day refill flow plus subscribe-and-save on the site.

## Best angle for the first line
"Dan, you've told the stress-type story on a dozen podcasts. Where do those listeners land when they go to buy?"

## Watch-outs
- Retail partners may resist a strong direct push; frame it as owning the podcast buyer, not undercutting stores.
- Email dan@reddremedies.com is pattern-guessed (finder spent $0): verify before sending.
